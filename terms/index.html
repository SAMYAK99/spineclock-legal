1. What SpineClock is

SpineClock is a workday break alarm. You set work hours, active days, and a focus interval. When an interval ends, the app can sound an alarm, turn the screen on, and keep the break visible until the phone counts the step goal you set. You can also do short chair, wall, or hallway resets, and the app can record streaks, badges, and an expedition trail.

You may use the basic timer without creating an account. Optional sign-in lets you keep progress if you change phones.
